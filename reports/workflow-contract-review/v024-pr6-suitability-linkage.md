## Generated argv Replay

PR6 does not add generated argv. It relies on PR5 collect-plan steps and checks
the artifact payload fields written by those typed commands. Suitability does
not infer storage/network/latency from directory co-presence, filename ordering,
mtime, or `latest` paths.

## Claim Boundaries

- `workflow.recommendation` and `workflow.collect_plan` remain workflow authority
  / handoff artifacts, not target measurement evidence.
- Counter-only network evidence remains unknown for required network
  suitability.
- Endpoint-backed bounded transfer is enough to avoid `unknown`, but not enough
  to claim network `meet` without policy thresholds.
- Tempfile/page-cache storage smoke is enough to avoid `unknown`, but not enough
  to claim storage-device `meet`.
- Current-condition latency jitter is enough to avoid `unknown`, but not enough
  to claim latency `meet` without workload latency thresholds.
- Unknown required dimensions continue to block `selection_ready`.

## Forbidden Fallback Checks

- No filename-order selection.
- No mtime/latest/newest artifact inference.
- No network meet from rx/tx counters or counter-only evidence.
- No storage-device meet from tempfile/page-cache-only smoke.
- No selection readiness when a required dimension remains unknown.
